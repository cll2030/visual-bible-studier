What the words meant

Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek behind the English
Studies are written by Claude from general scholarship and can contain mistakes, especially in dates, coordinates of uncertain sites and vowel pointing. Check anything you plan to teach against a lexicon and a commentary. Finished studies go into a shared library, so each passage is written once and then opens for everyone, even offline once opened on this device. Verse wording follows the World English Bible (public domain). The figures are schematic drawings built from a small set of garment shapes, so they show the kind of clothing, not exact cut or pattern. Coastlines, lakes and rivers are modern, from Natural Earth.
